The evidence, with sources you can check

Every statistic links to the UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) or the World Resources Institute (WRI). Open the sources, read the context, and question the numbers.

~1/3

Agrifood emissions

FAO estimates agrifood systems account for about one-third of global human-caused greenhouse-gas emissions.

Source: UN FAO
8–10%

Food loss and waste

WRI estimates food loss and waste contribute 8–10% of annual global greenhouse-gas emissions.

Source: WRI
3.1 Gt

Tree-cover loss

WRI reports that global tree-cover loss in 2024 caused 3.1 gigatonnes of greenhouse-gas emissions.

Source: WRI

Myth or fact?

Tap each card to reveal the answer.

“Cold weather means global warming is not happening.”

Myth

Weather is short-term; climate is the long-term trend.

“Food waste has little climate impact.”

Myth

WRI estimates it causes 8–10% of global emissions.

Source: WRI

“Forests influence climate by storing carbon.”

Fact

Forests remove CO₂, while deforestation releases stored carbon.

Source: WRI
